# LuxeBot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 16, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LuxeBot ("we", "our", "the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your data when you use our Discord bot and dashboard.

## 2. Data We Collect

When you add LuxeBot to your server, we collect and store:

- **Server data:** Server ID, server name, channel IDs, role IDs, and bot configuration settings
- **User data:** User IDs, message counts, XP and level data, moderation history (warns, mutes, bans)
- **Ticket data:** Ticket content and logs for servers using the ticket system
- **Custom commands:** Any custom commands or reaction roles configured by server administrators
- **Voice activity:** Voice channel join/leave timestamps for voice XP tracking

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data exclusively to:

- Provide and operate LuxeBot's features (leveling, moderation, tickets, etc.)
- Track XP, levels, and leaderboard rankings
- Store server configuration and settings
- Process moderation actions and maintain logs
- Manage premium subscriptions and trial periods

## 4. Data Storage

All data is stored in a SQLite database hosted on Railway. Data is retained for as long as LuxeBot remains in your server. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

## 5. Data Deletion

When LuxeBot is removed from your server, you may request deletion of all associated data by contacting us through our support server. Server administrators can also reset specific data using bot commands.

## 6. Third-Party Services

LuxeBot integrates with:

- **Discord API** — to operate as a bot within Discord servers
- **Whop** — to process premium subscription payments
- **top.gg** — for bot listing and voting

Each service has its own privacy policy that governs their use of your data.

## 7. Dashboard

When you log into the LuxeBot dashboard using Discord OAuth2, we access your Discord username, avatar, and server list to display your dashboard. We do not store your Discord OAuth2 tokens permanently.

## 8. Children's Privacy

LuxeBot does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of LuxeBot after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, join our support server at https://discord.gg/saTswBaM or visit https://luxebot-production.up.railway.app.
